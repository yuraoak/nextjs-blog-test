## A small test

Open a note you wrote five years ago in whatever app you used at the time. Does it still render? Can you copy it out cleanly?

If the answer is no, the tool failed you.

## Plain text wins by default

Plain text — and its slightly fancier cousin, markdown — has a few properties that matter on long timescales:

- **It's diff-friendly.** Git handles it natively.
- **It's grep-friendly.** You can search a decade of notes in a second.
- **It outlives the tool that made it.** No vendor lock-in.

## What I keep in markdown

- Blog posts (obviously)
- Meeting notes
- Reading highlights
- Half-formed product ideas

Fancy editors come and go. The files stay.
