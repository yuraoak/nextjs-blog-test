## Why a blog, again

I've started and abandoned a dozen blogs. Each time I picked a stack that was too clever, and the maintenance cost killed the writing habit.

This time the rule is simple: **plain markdown files, no database, no CMS**. If I can't write a post by opening a `.md` file in my editor, the system is wrong.

## What lives here

Short notes, mostly:

- things I'm building
- code I keep relearning
- product decisions I want to remember

Nothing here is meant to be a definitive guide. It's a public notebook.

> The best blog is the one you actually update.
